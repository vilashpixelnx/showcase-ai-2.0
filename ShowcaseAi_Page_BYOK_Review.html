READ FIRST · APPLIES TO EVERY PAGE

3 rules for the whole funnel

1. Kill all "credit" language. The whole funnel is credit-based. Under BYOK the buyer connects their own AI access = unlimited, so no tier can sell "more credits." Differentiate every tier by features / quality instead.
2. Add the "unlimited" angle wherever a page says "no monthly fee": "No monthly fees. No credit limits. Create as much as you want." Several pages already say "unlimited" — BYOK finally makes those claims true.
3. Soft on the mechanism, honest in the FAQ. Don't shout "bring your own API key" (it scares beginners). Lead with the OUTCOME (unlimited); say "connected straight to the AI / quick one-time setup" in the copy — and disclose it clearly in ONE place: the FAQ (so it's not a bait-and-switch). And never write "free / pennies per video" until the dev confirms the real cost.
